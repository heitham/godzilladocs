Rate Limits

Rate limits are enforced per API key and reset every minute. Exceeding a rate limit returns a 429 Too Many Requests response.

Plan Tier Rate Limit Burst Allowance
Starter 1,000 req/min Up to 2,000 in a single second
Professional 10,000 req/min Up to 20,000 in a single second
Enterprise 50,000 req/min Up to 100,000 in a single second
Custom Negotiated Negotiated
Rate Limit Headers
Every API response includes three headers that show your current usage: X-RateLimit-Limit, X-RateLimit-Remaining, and X-RateLimit-Reset. Use these to monitor your consumption in real time.

What Happens at Quota

When you reach your monthly request quota, your API key will stop accepting requests. All calls will receive a 403 Forbidden response with the message "Quota exceeded."

Monthly Quota Reset

Monthly quotas reset automatically on the first day of your billing cycle (usually the date you signed up). You can view your current billing cycle and usage in the Billing section of your account dashboard.

Soft Limit Warning

When you reach 80% of your monthly quota, we send an email warning to your account owner. This gives you time to assess your usage before hitting the hard limit.

Exceeding Your Quota

If you consistently exceed your quota:

  1. Upgrade your plan: Visit your Billing dashboard to upgrade to a higher tier with a larger monthly allocation and higher rate limits.
  2. Optimize your usage: Review your API calls and remove unnecessary requests. Consider batching operations or using webhooks instead of polling.
  3. Request a custom plan: If none of our standard tiers fit your needs, contact our sales team to discuss a custom Enterprise plan.
Requests During Overage
Requests made after reaching your quota will not be processed. There is no "overdraft" or carry-forward mechanism. Plan your usage accordingly, and upgrade in advance if you anticipate a spike in traffic.

Monitoring Usage

You can monitor your real-time API usage in two ways:

Dashboard Metrics

Log into your account dashboard and navigate to Usage & Analytics. You'll see:

  • Requests made this month vs. your quota
  • Average requests per day and per hour
  • Peak usage times
  • Breakdown by endpoint

Response Headers

Every API response includes rate-limit headers. Parse these in your client code to track remaining quota:

Example bash 
curl -i https://api.godzilla.dev/v1/pipelines/list \\\n  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"\n\nHTTP/1.1 200 OK\nX-RateLimit-Limit: 1000\nX-RateLimit-Remaining: 987\nX-RateLimit-Reset: 1699564800

When X-RateLimit-Remaining approaches zero, your next request may be rejected with a 429 response. Plan ahead and either wait for the reset or upgrade your plan.

Pro Tip
Implement exponential backoff and jitter in your retry logic to gracefully handle rate-limit responses. See our Handling Rate Limits guide for code examples.
