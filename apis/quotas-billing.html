Quotas & billing Reference

The Godzilla platform offers tiered subscription plans with distinct rate limits, event quotas, and storage allocations. This page covers plan features, quota enforcement, and billing behavior when limits are exceeded.

Plan tiers

Godzilla subscriptions are organized by usage tier, each with defined resource limits.

Tier Requests / minute Events / month Storage Support
Starter 500 100,000 10 GB Community
Professional 1,000 1,000,000 100 GB Email
Enterprise Custom Custom Custom Dedicated

Quota enforcement

Godzilla enforces quotas in real-time across your workspace. The specific behavior depends on which quota type is exceeded:

Rate limits

When you exceed your tier's requests-per-minute limit, Godzilla returns 429 Too Many Requests. See Rate limits & errors for header details and retry guidance.

rate-limit-response.json json 
{\n  \"error\": {\n    \"code\": \"rate_limit_exceeded\",\n    \"message\": \"Rate limit of 1,000 requests/minute exceeded. Retry after 60 seconds.\",\n    \"request_id\": \"req_a1b2c3\"\n  }\n}

Event quota

Events are counted against your monthly quota starting on the first calendar day of each month. Once your tier's monthly event limit is reached, new events are rejected with a 402 Payment Required status.

Quota reset timing

Monthly event quotas reset at midnight UTC on the first of each month, regardless of when your subscription began. Enterprise accounts may have custom reset dates — contact support to arrange this.

When you hit the event quota:

quota-exceeded-response.json json 
{\n  \"error\": {\n    \"code\": \"event_quota_exceeded\",\n    \"message\": \"Event quota of 1,000,000 events/month exhausted. Upgrade your plan or wait for the quota to reset on September 1, 2026.\",\n    \"request_id\": \"req_x9y8z7\",\n    \"quota\": {\n      \"limit\": 1000000,\n      \"used\": 1000000,\n      \"reset_date\": \"2026-09-01T00:00:00Z\"\n    }\n  }\n}

Storage quota

When stored data (including event history, connector metadata, and pipeline logs) exceeds your tier's storage limit, Godzilla enters read-only mode: existing pipelines can fetch data, but new events, updates, and configuration changes are blocked until you free up space or upgrade.

Storage limits are strict

Unlike rate-limited or event-quota scenarios, storage-full conditions prevent all writes to your workspace until you either delete old data or upgrade to a plan with more capacity.

Overage billing and upgrade options

If you temporarily exceed a quota, you have several options:

  • Upgrade your plan: Move to a higher tier at any time during your billing cycle. Your new limits take effect immediately, and you'll be pro-rated for the remainder of the month.
  • Purchase add-on capacity: For Professional and Enterprise plans, buy additional event quotas (sold in 500,000-event blocks) or storage (sold in 50 GB blocks) without committing to a full plan upgrade.
  • Contact sales: If you're on a custom Enterprise plan or need a one-time increase, reach out to your account manager.

Monitoring usage

Your workspace dashboard displays real-time usage across all quotas:

  • Rate limit: Current requests in the current minute (reset per X-RateLimit-Reset).
  • Event quota: Events consumed this calendar month, with reset countdown.
  • Storage: Total bytes stored, displayed as a percentage of your tier's limit.

The dashboard also sends alerts when you reach 80%, 95%, and 100% of any quota. Email and webhook notifications are available for Professional and Enterprise subscribers.

Custom limits for Enterprise

Enterprise plans can negotiate custom rate limits, event quotas, storage allocations, and billing schedules. Your dedicated account manager will provision these limits and configure any custom reset dates or billing cycles.
