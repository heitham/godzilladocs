Quotas & billing v2

Godzilla offers tiered plans with different request rate limits and features. Your plan determines how many API requests your workspace can make per minute, and what happens when you exceed that limit.

Plan tiers

Choose the plan that fits your usage. All plans include full API access to pipelines, connectors, transforms, events, and webhooks.

What happens when you exceed your quota

\n\n

When your workspace reaches its rate limit, new API requests receive a 429 Too Many Requests response.

\n\n{{ds:component/code-block|filename=rate-limit-response.json|language=json|code=HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests\nX-RateLimit-Limit: 1000\nX-RateLimit-Remaining: 0\nX-RateLimit-Reset: 1693430400\nRetry-After: 60\n\n{\n "error": {\n "code": "rate_limit_exceeded",\n "message": "Rate limit of 1000 requests per minute exceeded. Try again after 60 seconds.",\n "request_id": "req_a1b2c3d4"\n }\n}}}\n\n

Response headers

\n\n

Every API response includes three rate limit headers:

\n\n
    \n
  • X-RateLimit-Limit: Your workspace's requests-per-minute limit.
    • \n
  • X-RateLimit-Remaining: How many requests you have left in the current minute.
    • \n
  • X-RateLimit-Reset: Unix timestamp when the limit resets.
    • \n
\n\n

When a request is rejected for exceeding the limit, you'll also see a Retry-After header indicating how many seconds to wait before retrying.

\n\n

Best practices

\n\n
    \n
  • Monitor your headers: Check X-RateLimit-Remaining before hitting the limit. When it drops below 10% of your quota, consider slowing your request rate.
    • \n
  • Use exponential backoff: If you hit a 429, wait the duration in Retry-After, then retry. If you hit another 429, double the wait time.
    • \n
  • Batch operations: Group multiple operations into a single API call when possible (e.g., create multiple transforms in one request).
    • \n
  • Upgrade your plan: If you consistently hit your limit, your usage may have outgrown your current plan. Upgrade to the next tier or contact sales for a custom arrangement.
    • \n
\n\n

Upgrading your plan

\n\n

To upgrade or change plans, visit your account settings and select a new plan under Billing. Changes take effect immediately, and you'll be prorated based on your current billing cycle.

\n\n

For Enterprise plans or custom rate limits, contact our sales team.
