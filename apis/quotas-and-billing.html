Quotas & billing reference

The Godzilla platform offers multiple plan tiers, each with distinct API request quotas and billing implications. This page covers how quotas work, what happens when you exceed them, and how to choose or upgrade your plan.

Plan tiers

Quotas are enforced per workspace and reset monthly on your billing anniversary.

Tracking your quota usage

Every API response includes quota-tracking headers:

response-headers text 
X-RateLimit-Limit: 1000
X-RateLimit-Remaining: 987
X-RateLimit-Reset: 2026-09-15T12:00:00Z
X-Quota-Limit: 10000000
X-Quota-Remaining: 9,847,329
X-Quota-Reset: 2026-09-20T14:30:00Z

Use X-Quota-Remaining to proactively check your monthly allowance and X-Quota-Reset to see when it renews.

What happens when you exceed your quota

Rate-limit vs. quota exceedances

Rate limit (per minute): Returns 429 Too Many Requests immediately. Respect Retry-After and back off. See Rate limits & errors.

Monthly quota (per billing period): Once exhausted, all API calls fail with a 402 Payment Required error until the quota resets or your plan is upgraded.

429 Too Many Requests (rate limit)

You've exceeded the per-minute rate limit for your plan tier. The response includes a Retry-After header:

rate-limit-response.json json 
{
  "error": {
    "code": "rate_limit_exceeded",
    "message": "Rate limit exceeded. Retry after 60 seconds.",
    "request_id": "req_a1b2c3"
  }
}

Action: Wait the duration specified in Retry-After (in seconds), then retry your request. Implement exponential backoff for resilience. See Handling errors & retries for detailed strategies.

402 Payment Required (quota exceeded)

Your workspace has exhausted its monthly quota. All API requests return:

quota-exceeded-response.json json 
{
  "error": {
    "code": "quota_exceeded",
    "message": "Monthly request quota exhausted. Quota resets on 2026-09-20.",
    "request_id": "req_d4e5f6"
  }
}

Action: Either wait until your quota resets on the date shown, or upgrade your plan immediately. You cannot retry during a quota exceedance — the error will persist for all requests until the quota renews or you upgrade.

Upgrading your plan

Upgrade at any time during your billing cycle by visiting your workspace settings or contacting sales for Enterprise tiers. Quota increases take effect immediately; any overage during the current month incurs immediate charges at the new plan's rate.

Proactive monitoring

Check X-Quota-Remaining regularly and set up alerts within your application or monitoring tools to trigger plan upgrades before exhaustion. Many teams automate this check in their error handlers.

Billing and invoices

Monthly invoices are generated on your billing anniversary based on:

  • Plan tier — Base price per your chosen plan (Starter, Standard, Enterprise).
  • Overage charges — If you exceed your quota during a billing period, you are charged per request above the limit until upgrade or period reset.
  • Usage analytics — Detailed breakdowns by workspace, endpoint, and date are available in your account dashboard.

Invoices are sent to your registered billing email; download them directly from the Godzilla dashboard under Billing > Invoices.
