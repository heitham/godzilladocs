Quotas & billing

Godzilla's pricing is based on usage tiers. Every workspace has a quota based on its current plan, and requests that exceed that quota are rate-limited.

Plan tiers

Each plan comes with a distinct request quota and set of features:

Name Type Required Description
Starter100 requests / minuteBasic API access, 5 pipelines max$29 / month
Standard1,000 requests / minuteFull API access, unlimited pipelines, custom connectors$149 / month
EnterpriseCustom limitSLA, dedicated support, custom integrationsContact sales

What happens when you exceed your quota

When your workspace exceeds its plan's request rate limit, subsequent requests receive a 429 Too Many Requests response. The response includes:

  • Retry-After header — how many seconds to wait before retrying
  • X-RateLimit-Reset header — Unix timestamp when the quota window resets
  • X-RateLimit-Remaining header — requests left in the current window
rate-limit-response.json json 
HTTP/1.1 429 Too Many Requests\nRetry-After: 60\nX-RateLimit-Remaining: 0\nX-RateLimit-Reset: 1693546800\n\n{\n  "error": {\n    "code": "rate_limit_exceeded",\n    "message": "Rate limit exceeded. Please try again after 60 seconds.\"\n  }\n}
Upgrade available

If your workspace consistently hits rate limits, consider upgrading to a higher tier to increase your quota. Log in to your account dashboard to review usage and upgrade options.

Monitoring usage

Check your current usage and remaining quota via the account dashboard or by inspecting rate-limit headers on any API response:

  • X-RateLimit-Limit — your plan's total request quota per minute
  • X-RateLimit-Remaining — requests left in the current minute window

Use the Retry-After header to implement intelligent backoff. For details on request structure and error responses, see Rate limits & errors.

Billing and invoicing

Invoices are generated monthly on your subscription anniversary date. Your billing email receives notifications when:

  • A new invoice is issued
  • Payment fails and needs a retry
  • Your plan is about to renew

You can update your billing email, payment method, and plan at any time from the account settings.
