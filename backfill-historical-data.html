Backfill historical data

This recipe shows you how to load historical data into your destination connector using Godzilla's batch operations and event replay capabilities.

Overview

Backfilling historical data is a common task when setting up a new data pipeline. You may want to:

  • Migrate existing data from a legacy system
  • Initialize a new destination with historical records
  • Recover from a pipeline failure by replaying events

Getting started

To backfill data, you'll need:

  • A source connector with historical data available
  • A destination connector ready to receive events
  • Access to the Godzilla API to manage the pipeline

Step 1: Prepare your source

Ensure your source connector has all the historical data you want to backfill. Most connectors support pagination or cursor-based iteration to retrieve older records.

Step 2: Create or update your pipeline

Set up a pipeline that connects your source to your destination. You can do this through the Godzilla UI or via the API.

Step 3: Backfill data

Once your pipeline is configured, trigger a full sync to backfill all historical data. Depending on your connector, this might happen automatically on first run or require explicit triggering.

Step 4: Monitor progress

Use the Godzilla monitoring dashboard to track the progress of your backfill operation. Events will flow through your pipeline transforms and into your destination.

Best practices

  • Start small: Test with a small subset of historical data first to ensure your pipeline is configured correctly.
  • Handle duplicates: Design your destination to handle potential duplicate records, as replay or retries may send the same event multiple times.
  • Monitor throughput: Be aware of rate limits on both your source and destination connectors.
  • Plan for large backfills: If you're backfilling a very large amount of data, consider breaking it into smaller batches.
