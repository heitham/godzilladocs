Quotas & billing

Godzilla accounts operate under plan-based quotas. This page describes each plan tier, monthly request and storage allowances, and the behavior when you exceed your quota.

Plan tiers

Choose the plan that fits your volume and team size:

Starter (Free)

  • Rate limit: 1,000 requests per minute
  • Monthly events: 10 million
  • Storage: 1 GB event history
  • Connectors: Up to 5
  • Support: Community forums

Professional

  • Rate limit: 10,000 requests per minute
  • Monthly events: 500 million
  • Storage: 100 GB event history
  • Connectors: Unlimited
  • Support: Email support, 24-hour response time
  • Pricing: $499 / month

Enterprise

  • Rate limit: Custom (typically 100,000+ requests per minute)
  • Monthly events: Unlimited
  • Storage: Unlimited
  • Connectors: Unlimited
  • Support: Dedicated support engineer, SLA guarantee
  • Pricing: Custom quote

When you exceed your quota

If your account exceeds its plan quota, the following behavior applies:

Rate limit exceeded

429 Too Many Requests

When your account's per-minute rate limit is reached, all subsequent requests within that minute window return HTTP 429 Too Many Requests. The response includes a Retry-After header indicating how many seconds to wait before retrying. See Limits, quotas & errors for details on monitoring your usage.

Monthly event quota exceeded

Event ingestion paused

Once your monthly event quota is reached, no new events can be ingested into any pipeline in your account until the quota resets on the first day of the next month. Existing pipelines remain queryable and continue to process already-ingested events.

You'll receive an email alert when you reach 80% of your monthly quota, giving you time to upgrade your plan or wait for the monthly reset.

Event storage quota exceeded

Oldest events archived

When your account's total event storage reaches its quota, the oldest events are automatically moved to cold storage (archived). Archived events are no longer queryable via the API but remain available for export or restoration within 90 days. After 90 days, archived events are permanently deleted.

Upgrading your plan

Need more capacity? Upgrade your plan from your account settings:

  1. Log in to your Godzilla account
  2. Navigate to Settings > Billing
  3. Click Upgrade plan
  4. Select your new plan and complete payment
  5. Your new limits take effect immediately

For Enterprise plans, contact sales to discuss a custom configuration.

Overage pricing

The Professional plan includes overage charges if you exceed monthly event limits:

  • Professional plan: $0.001 per additional million events (after 500M)
  • Enterprise plan: Included in contract (no overage charges)

Overage charges are billed at the end of each month and appear on your next invoice.
