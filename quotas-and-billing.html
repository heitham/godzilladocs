Quotas & billing

Every Godzilla account operates under a plan tier, which determines API request quotas, data retention limits, and billing charges. This page explains each tier and what happens when you exceed your quota.

What happens when you exceed your quota

When your account reaches its quota limit, the API behaves differently depending on the type of limit that was exceeded:

Rate limit (requests per minute)

When you exceed your per-minute rate limit, the API returns a 429 Too Many Requests status code. The response includes a Retry-After header indicating how many seconds to wait before retrying. See Rate limit headers in the API reference for details on tracking your usage with response headers.

Action to take: Implement exponential backoff in your client to automatically retry failed requests after the delay specified in Retry-After. If you consistently hit the rate limit, consider upgrading your plan tier.

Daily quota exceeded

Once you exceed your daily request quota, all API requests return a 429 Too Many Requests status with a message indicating the daily quota was exceeded. Your quota resets at midnight UTC each day.

Action to take: Wait for the quota reset at midnight UTC, or upgrade to a higher plan tier with a larger daily quota. Contact support if you need a temporary quota increase.

Data retention limit exceeded

Event data older than your plan's retention period is automatically deleted and cannot be recovered. For example, Starter-tier accounts only retain 7 days of event history.

Action to take: If you need to keep historical event data longer, export it before the retention window expires, or upgrade to a plan tier with extended retention. Standard tier retains data for 30 days.

Monitor your usage

Check your workspace settings or billing dashboard regularly to monitor your API usage, remaining quota, and data retention status. Set up alerts in your monitoring system to notify you when you approach a quota limit.

Upgrading your plan

To upgrade from Starter to Standard, or to discuss custom limits for an Enterprise plan:

  1. Log in to your workspace at https://app.godzilladocs.dev
  2. Go to Settings → Billing
  3. Choose your new plan and complete the payment process

For Enterprise plans or custom quota requests, contact our sales team.

Frequently asked questions

Do I get charged for failed requests?

No. Requests that return an error (including 429 rate-limit responses) do not count against your quota or billing.

Can I get a temporary quota increase?

Yes. Contact support with details about your use case, and we can discuss a temporary increase or recommend the right plan tier for your needs.

What is the monthly billing cycle?

Billing cycles run from the 1st to the last day of each calendar month. Charges appear on the billing method on file shortly after the end of the cycle. For annual plans, billing runs on the anniversary of your purchase date.

Can I downgrade my plan?

Yes. You can downgrade anytime from your billing settings, and the change takes effect on your next billing cycle. Please note that downgrading may reduce your rate limits and data retention.
